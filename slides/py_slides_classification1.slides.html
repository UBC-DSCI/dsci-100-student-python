Question¶

Suppose we have a new observation in the iris dataset, with

  • petal length = 5
  • petal width = 0.6

Discuss the following questions with your peers:

  • Create a plot to visualize the relationship between the predictors/features. Based on your plot, how would you classify this observation based on $k=3$ nearest neighbours?
  • Do you think we need to scale the data? Why or why not?

Answer¶

The 3 nearest neighbours to the new observation are all versicolor. Therefore, using (k=3), we would classify the new observation as versicolor.

Should we scale? Yes, it would be a good idea.

  • Petal length and petal width are both measured in cm, but they have different ranges.
  • Petal length ranges roughly from 1 to 7 cm, while petal width ranges roughly from 0.1 to 2.5 cm.
  • Without scaling, differences in petal length have more influence on the distance calculation.
  • Scaling puts the predictors on comparable scales so that one feature does not dominate the K-NN distance simply because of its magnitude.
